The problem

A memory that only adds will eventually lie to you.

People change. Most assistants just pile new facts on top of old ones, so yesterday's you keeps voting on today's decisions. Decisive gives every fact a life, and only the living ones get a say.

How it thinks

Four moves, and nothing is ever deleted.

The memory is a graph, so every fact knows what replaced it, what it was built on, and which decisions used it.

Remember

Say things in plain words. Each becomes a dated fact: a diet, a goal, a friend, a promise.

Replace

When something changes, the old fact is kept as history and marked replaced. Retract a mistake and everything built on it goes too.

Decide

Only facts that are true now reach the decision, weighed by rules you can read and by how past choices turned out.

Prove

Every answer links to the facts it used and the path through the graph. Rewind to any day to see what it believed.

What you get

Six things most assistants cannot do.

◉

Only what is true now

✎

◷

Time travel

Drag a slider to any day and see what it believed then, and why it answered the way it did.

↺

Say a fact was wrong and it disappears along with every conclusion that depended on it.

Judgment about people

Advice on friendships grounded in patterns, promises and your own values, not flattery.
